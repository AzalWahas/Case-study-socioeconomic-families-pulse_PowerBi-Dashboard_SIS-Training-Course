# Families Pulse Dashboard

<img width="1371" height="755" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d0724956-566d-4175-91aa-9c74d1cb1282" />

A comprehensive Power BI reporting solution designed to track core household demographics, family distributions, employment statistics, and economic indicators across regions.

---

## 📂 Dashboard Overview & Metrics

* **Summary KPI Cards**: 
  * **11,905 Members**: Total individual members across all recorded households.
  * **1,701 Families**: Total active family units tracked in the dataset.
  * **$143,339 Total Income**: Aggregated financial income across monitored households.

---

## 📊 Detailed Visualizations & Breakdowns

### 1. Geographic & Regional Distribution
* **Families by Country**: Column chart displaying total family units per country (Syria: 647, Yemen: 606, Iraq: 224, Libya: 224).
* **Number of Cities per Country**: Donut chart outlining city distribution percentages across Yemen (32%), Syria (26%), Iraq (21%), and Libya (21%) totaling 19 cities.

### 2. Employment & Socio-Economic Analysis
* **Families with Mother Without Job by Country**: Breakdown tracking unemployed mother counts across regional sectors (Syria: 133, Yemen: 106, Iraq: 58, Libya: 45).

### 3. Demographics & Housing
* **PCT of Males vs Females**: Donut breakdown displaying gender representation across members (55% Females vs 45% Males).
* **Families by House**: Funnel/bar distribution categorizing living conditions into Flat (627), Room (587), Tent (344), and House (143).

---

## 🛠️ Global Filters & Navigation

* **Global Slicers**: Interactive top-level filters allowing real-time cross-filtering across the report by **Country**, **House**, and **Breadwinner**.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Prerequisites**: Ensure you have Power BI Desktop installed.
2. **Open Project**: Load the `.pbix` report file to initialize the underlying data model and visual layers.
3. **Explore**: Use the top global slicers to filter data dynamically by country, housing type, and breadwinner status.
